Accounting & Advisory

Richard Munro

Richard Munro is a CPA who, through his advisory practice Alternate Business Strategies, draws on his own experience building and exiting a financial services business to guide other business owners. His work centres on employee engagement through share ownership, business structure, financial systems and leadership. A past President of CPA Australia Gold Coast, he is also active in the local arts community as Treasurer of the Gold Coast Eisteddfod and a board member of the Arts Gold Coast Foundation.

Matthew Munro

Matthew Munro is a senior software developer and AI engineer with over 20 years of experience shipping production software, including a decade co-founding and running his own software company. His work focuses on the engineering around large language models — evaluation, guardrails and tooling — with an agentic AI-assisted workflow at the core of how he builds. Through Munro Solutions he also makes and ships his own games, including Floodline, released solo on the App Store and Google Play.
